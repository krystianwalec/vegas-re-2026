St. Thomas project

Overall system picture

How the Tuesday meetings fit together — one stack per house, unless a shared plant turns out to be smarter. Andy will bring the home plans so vendors can talk sizing, wind calcs, and attachments from real drawings.

Three new ~4,000 sq ft rental homes on St. Thomas, USVI. Target: solar + storage covering 80–90% of load. Flat roofs, hurricane and salt-air exposure, owners off-island (remote monitoring and service matter). Andy’s company, Custom Unique Homes Inc., is managing the work. A separate ask — Krystian’s Bellevue house — is backup batteries only, with no solar because of trees.

1. Roof & racking

Hurricane-rated flat-roof mounting — Enstall (PanelClaw clawFR or an attached alternative). Tesla Solar Roof is unlikely on flat, coastal roofs; confirm before spending time on it.

2. Panels

Conventional PV modules, specified by the installer. No module-maker meeting is booked; this sits with ProSolar or another STT EPC.

3. Inverter / hybrid brain

Pick one primary path — these compete: Tesla Powerwall 3, Sol-Ark 15K, Victron Quattro / MultiPlus-II, SMA Sunny Boy Smart Energy, or Enphase IQ8.

4. Battery

Usually bundled with that path (Powerwall, IQ Battery, SMA battery, PWRcell, FranklinWH aPower) or a 48V pack paired with Sol-Ark or Victron.

5. Monitoring

Must work from off-island: Tesla app, Victron VRM, Sol-Ark app, Sunny Portal, Enphase, or FranklinWH EMS. Reliable internet on STT is part of the design.

6. Installer & WAPA

Local installer (ProSolar already does Tesla and Enphase on STT) handles shipping, DPNR permitting, WAPA interconnection, and service. Systems over 10 kWac typically fall under WAPA Net Energy Billing, not 1:1 net metering. A generator is optional backup for outages.

Compete vs complement: Enstall complements every conventional-PV path. ProSolar is the local installer, not a competing equipment brand. Tesla, Sol-Ark, Victron, SMA, and Enphase are alternative “brains + storage” stacks — leave Tuesday with one shortlist, not three overlapping systems. FranklinWH and Generac are storage (and Generac generator-hybrid) alternatives. Bellevue is a separate, backup-only conversation with Sol-Ark and the walk-ups.
